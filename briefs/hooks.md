# Hook bank for Your Times reels

One hook = first line of a reel. Rotate, never reuse within 14 days. Each reel: 4–6 lines, 12–18 seconds, last line is the CTA.
Never put a price in a video. CTA options: "Want your own? Link in bio." / "Yours is in the bio." / "Link in bio if you want one."

## frontpage (masthead + headlines appear under the voice)
- This newspaper has exactly one reader.
- My front page this morning: three people, one payment, zero news.
- An AI wrote a newspaper about my Tuesday.
- Here's what made my front page today.
- My inbox became a newspaper. Front page only.
- 47 messages came in overnight. Three made the paper.
- The headline this morning was my mom.
- Nothing happened today. Best edition so far.
- My paper has a payments column. Today it says: $1,240, one failed.
- Every morning the front page tells me who's waiting.

## printer (page comes out of the printer)
- Six thirty. The printer wakes up before I do.
- This is the only thing I print.
- My printer knows my day before I do.
- One page a day. That's the whole system.
- I stopped checking my phone. The printer checks it for me.
- The sound of my morning: one page, 40 seconds.
- Printed for one reader. Me.
- Paper beats push notifications. Here's mine.

## lockscreen (notifications clear, the paper stays)
- This is what my phone used to look like at 6:30.
- Forty notifications before coffee. Not anymore.
- I deleted nothing. I just stopped seeing it first.
- My lock screen is a newspaper now.
- Same phone. Same inbox. Different morning.
- Every app wants me at 6:30. Only one page gets me.
- The feed decides your morning. Mine is decided at midnight.
- Here's my phone before and after an AI read it for me.

## versus (their morning vs mine)
- Their morning: the feed decides what matters.
- Two mornings. Same amount of information.
- One of us scrolled for 25 minutes. One of us read a page.
- You check twelve apps. I check one page.
- Doomscrolling vs. a newspaper about me.
- Breaking news vs. my news.

## headlines (one huge headline per line)
- My newspaper wrote these headlines about me today.
- Local woman answers all three emails. Nation stunned.
- Obituary for the task I kept postponing.
- Horoscope that only knows my calendar.
- Weather: 29 and clear. Inbox: 47 and cloudy.
- Front page: the invoice got paid. Page two: nothing.

## haunted (October 20–31 only, theme: haunted)
- My newspaper went haunted this morning.
- The Haunted Edition printed an obituary for my deadlines.
- A horoscope that knew too much.
- Local woman survives 47 unread emails.
- Tonight's forecast: a payment that never arrived.
- Page two of the Haunted Edition is a séance with my inbox.
